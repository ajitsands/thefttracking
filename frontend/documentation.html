1. The 4 Core AI Behavior Roles

AegisVision organizes camera surveillance into 4 specialized behavior zone roles. Each role fulfills a distinct purpose in the theft detection state-machine:

Shelf Zone

1. Shelf Pick Zone

Monitors product display shelves, hanging racks, and item showcases. Detects the precise moment a customer's hand reaches in and picks an item.

Visual Color: Amber / Orange
Role Purpose: Item Pick Initiation
State Flag: shelf_touch_active
Recommended Area: Shelves & Racks
Concealment ROI

2. Pocket / Concealment ROI

Placed around customer waistlines, coat pockets, personal backpacks, or shopping bags. Detects sequential transfers where an item is hidden after being picked.

Visual Color: Crimson Red
Role Purpose: Theft Alert & Proof
Event Type: CONCEALMENT_DETECTED
Customizable Window: 1.0s – 15.0s (Default: 3.0s)
Checkout

3. Checkout Scanner Plane

Positioned across barcode scanner plates, conveyor belts, and cashier payment desks. Verifies item registration and flags "Sweethearting" (unscanned items passed across).

Visual Color: Cyan Blue
Role Purpose: Purchase Verification
Alert Flag: Passed Without Scan
Placement: Scanner Surface
Blindspot

4. Loitering Blind Spot

Calibrated over high-value jewelry/cosmetics cases or secluded aisle corners. Tracks dwelling duration to catch barcode peeling or anti-theft tag tampering.

Visual Color: Purple / Magenta
Role Purpose: Dwell & Tamper Alert
Event Type: LOITERING_SUSPICIOUS
Default Threshold: > 8.0 Seconds

2. Sequential Theft Detection Workflow

The vision engine does not produce false alarms on normal customer browsing. It executes a deterministic sequential state-machine:

Step 1: Item Pick
Motion in Shelf Zone
Step 2: Trajectory Timing
User Window (1s – 15s)
Step 3: Concealment
Motion into Pocket/Bag
Step 4: Evidence Vault
Snapshot & 4s MP4 Clip
🛡️ Zero False Alarm Principle: If a customer reaches into a shelf and puts the item in their shopping basket or hands it to a companion without moving into the red Concealment ROI, no theft alert is triggered.

3. 4-Corner Draggable Polygon ROI Calibration

Because real-world surveillance cameras are tilted at perspective angles, shelves are rarely perfect rectangles. AegisVision features an interactive 4-corner polygon calibration canvas:

4. Dynamic Multi-Factor Confidence Scoring

Rather than producing static or hardcoded percentages, the AI vision engine calculates dynamic confidence based on physical behavioral factors: 

# Multi-Factor Behavior Calculation (detection_engine.py)

1. Transfer Speed Factor:
   - 0.5s to 1.4s deliberate concealment  => Speed Score: 94%
   - Under 0.5s ultra-fast transfer       => Speed Score: 82%
   - 1.4s to 3.2s slower motion           => Speed Score: 65% - 88%

2. Motion Mass Consistency:
   - Area Score = clamp(Contour Area / 3000.0, 0.70, 1.0)

3. Sensitivity Scaling:
   - Sensitivity Multiplier = 0.60 + (UserSensitivity * 0.40)

Final Confidence = clamp((SpeedScore * 0.60 + AreaScore * 0.20) * SensitivityMultiplier, 68%, 98%)

5. IP Camera & RTSP Network Reference

Standard RTSP Stream URI formats for popular IP camera brands in supermarket installations:

Camera Brand Standard RTSP Stream Path Default Port
UNV (Uniview) ANPR rtsp://admin:PASSWORD@IP:554/Streaming/Channels/101 554 (TCP)
Hikvision / HiLook / CP Plus rtsp://admin:PASSWORD@IP:554/Streaming/Channels/101 554 (TCP)
Dahua / Amcrest / IMOU rtsp://admin:PASSWORD@IP:554/cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0 554 (TCP)
Generic ONVIF / XM rtsp://admin:PASSWORD@IP:554/live/ch0 or /onvif1 554 (TCP)
💡 Important: Special Characters in Passwords: If your camera password contains special characters like @ (e.g. admin@123), URL-encode the character as %40 (e.g. rtsp://admin:admin%40123@192.168.8.200:554/Streaming/Channels/101) so it does not conflict with the RTSP URL host separator.

6. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q: Why was there video delay and how was it resolved?

RTSP streams buffer frames in OpenCV FFMPEG if read synchronously. AegisVision utilizes a dedicated background thread (RealtimeCaptureReader) that drains incoming frames continuously, achieving instantaneous zero-lag live video.

Q: How do I arm the AI to begin recording theft incidents?

Click the AI: Standby / ARMED toggle button in the top navigation bar. When armed, the system will automatically generate evidence snapshots and 4-second MP4 video loops whenever a concealment transfer occurs.
