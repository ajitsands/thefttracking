The 4 Core AI Behavior Roles

Shelf Zone

Shelf Pick Zone

Monitors product showcases, hanging racks, and item display shelves. Detects the moment a customer's hand approaches and takes an item.

Visual Color: Yellow / Orange
Primary Purpose: Pick Item Initiation
Internal Flag: shelf_touch_active
Expected Placement: Display Case / Shelves
Concealment ROI

Pocket / Concealment ROI

Placed around waistlines, coat pockets, shopping bags, or personal backpacks. Detects sequential transfers where an item is hidden after being picked.

Visual Color: Crimson Red
Primary Purpose: Theft Detection & Proof
Trigger Event: CONCEALMENT_DETECTED
Window Period: 0.35s – 3.2s after pick
Checkout

Checkout Scanner Plane

Positioned across barcode scanner plates, conveyor belts, and cashier payment desks. Verifies item registration and flags "Sweethearting" (unscanned items).

Visual Color: Cyan Blue
Primary Purpose: Purchase Verification
Sweethearting Alert: Passed Without Scan
Expected Placement: Barcode Scanner Window
Blindspot

Loitering Blind Spot

Calibrated over high-value jewelry/cosmetics cases or secluded aisle corners. Tracks dwelling duration to catch barcode peeling or anti-theft tag tampering.

Visual Color: Purple / Magenta
Primary Purpose: Dwell & Tamper Alert
Trigger Event: LOITERING_SUSPICIOUS
Default Threshold: > 8.0 Seconds

4-Corner Draggable Polygon ROI Calibration

Real-world CCTV views have camera tilt, perspective distortion, and angled shelves. AegisVision features an interactive 4-corner polygon calibration tool:

Control Element Action Description & Behavior
Corner Pins (1, 2, 3, 4) Click & Drag Adjusts individual corner vertices (C1: Top-Left, C2: Top-Right, C3: Bottom-Right, C4: Bottom-Left) to form arbitrary quadrilaterals/trapezoids.
MOVE Anchor Pin Click & Drag Center Translates the entire zone across the camera view without altering its calibrated perspective angle.
Coordinate Input Boxes Manual Numeric Entry Precision pixel adjustment ($X: 0 - 640\text{px}$, $Y: 0 - 480\text{px}$) in the right-side inspector.
Point-in-Polygon Engine cv2.pointPolygonTest Evaluates exact geometric point-in-polygon containment for non-rectangular zones.

Dynamic Multi-Factor Confidence Scoring

Rather than producing static percentages, the AI vision engine calculates dynamic confidence based on physical behavioral factors: 

# Multi-Factor Behavior Calculation (detection_engine.py)
1. Transfer Speed Factor:
   - 0.5s to 1.4s deliberate concealment  => Speed Score: 94%
   - Under 0.5s ultra-fast transfer       => Speed Score: 82%
   - 1.4s to 3.2s slower motion           => Speed Score: 65% - 88%

2. Motion Mass Consistency:
   - Area Score = clamp(Contour Area / 3000.0, 0.70, 1.0)

3. Sensitivity Scaling:
   - Sensitivity Multiplier = 0.60 + (UserSensitivity * 0.40)

Final Confidence = clamp((SpeedScore * 0.60 + AreaScore * 0.20) * SensitivityMultiplier, 68%, 98%)
